About

I am a sociologist and postdoctoral researcher at the Department of Social Science at the University of Siegen. My research focuses on power relations in societies: How do they arise, and how do they become stable? Do all citizens have an equal chance to engage politically and use that influence to change institutional frameworks? And what role do differing socioeconomic starting points play?

Methodologically, I work with quantitative methods and the analysis of panel data. I studied sociology and business economics (B.A.) and sociology (M.A.) at the University of Mannheim and was a visiting researcher at the Department of Social Policy and Intervention at the University of Oxford (10/2023–03/2024).

Research

Research interests: Political sociology (poverty research, political participation) and quantitative methods.
